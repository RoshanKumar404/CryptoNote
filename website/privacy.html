Privacy Policy & Data Deletion

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 Application: CryptoNote for Android License: Open-Source (Apache 2.0)

1. Core Principle: Zero-Knowledge by Design

CryptoNote was engineered from the first line of code on a simple cryptographic truth: you own your keys, and only you should ever read your notes.

Unlike traditional cloud note applications that encrypt data only in transit or hold decryption keys on company servers, CryptoNote performs client-side authenticated encryption directly on your Android device before any byte leaves your phone.

Cryptographic Guarantee

Our sync servers only store opaque, encrypted binary envelopes (Envelope V1). We do not possess, cannot generate, and can never access your master passphrase, decryption keys, note titles, or note contents. Even if our servers were seized or compromised, your data remains mathematically indecipherable ciphertext.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the bare minimum information required to authenticate your account and sync your encrypted notes:

  • Account Credentials: Your registered email address and an Argon2id-derived authentication token used to authenticate sessions over TLS.
  • Encrypted Envelopes: Opaque binary payloads containing your AES-256-GCM encrypted notes, nonces, format version bytes, and cryptographic checksums.
  • Sync Sequence Metadata: Server-assigned monotonic version counters (sequence integers) and client device IDs used exclusively to ensure deterministic multi-device synchronization without conflicts.

What We NEVER Collect:

  • ✗ Plaintext Notes or Content: We never see your text, titles, markdown, checklists, or media.
  • ✗ Master Encryption Keys or Passphrases: Master keys are derived on-device and stored exclusively in the Android Keystore hardware module (TEE/StrongBox).
  • ✗ Biometric Data: Fingerprint and face authentication are processed entirely by Android's BiometricPrompt hardware API. Biometric data never leaves your hardware security enclave.
  • ✗ Location, Advertising, or Telemetry Data: No location tracking, no advertising identifiers, and no behavioral analytics SDKs are embedded in the app.

3. Security & Data Transmission

CryptoNote implements defense-in-depth security standards:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between your Android device and our backend servers are strictly enforced over modern HTTPS / TLS 1.3. Cleartext HTTP traffic is permanently blocked in the application manifest.
  • Encryption at Rest: Local storage utilizes encrypted SQLite databases and AES-256-GCM authenticated envelopes.
  • Anti-Forensic Memory Hygiene: Key buffers and passphrase character arrays in RAM are actively scrubbed and zeroized immediately after cryptographic derivation.
  • Screenshot & App Switcher Protection: When Vault Mode is enabled, the window is marked secure (FLAG_SECURE) to prevent unauthorized screen captures and task snapshots.

4. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

CryptoNote relies only on standard cloud infrastructure providers to run its sync servers:

  • Fly.io: Hosts our containerized Fastify API services in isolated regional microVMs.
  • PostgreSQL Database: Stores only user authentication records and opaque encrypted envelopes.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share any user data with advertisers, data brokers, or third-party marketing networks.

5. Account & Data Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policy and global privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA), you have the absolute right to delete your account and all associated data at any time.

Option A: In-App Instant Deletion (Self-Service)

You can permanently erase your account and all cloud data directly from your device:

  1. Open CryptoNote on your Android phone.
  2. Navigate to the Profile & Settings screen (tap your avatar icon in the top bar).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Erase & Reset Account".
  4. Confirm the prompt. Your local encryption keys, local database, and all cloud-synced envelopes will be instantaneously wiped from both your device and our production servers.

Option B: Web-Based Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or lost access to your device, you can request manual account deletion:

  1. Send an email to roshankuar951@gmail.com with the subject line CryptoNote Account Deletion Request.
  2. Include the registered email address associated with your CryptoNote account.
  3. Within 48 hours of receipt and identity confirmation, all account credentials, authentication records, and encrypted note envelopes associated with your email will be permanently purged from our production databases.

6. Children's Privacy

CryptoNote does not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.
