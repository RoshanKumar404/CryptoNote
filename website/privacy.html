Privacy Policy & Data Deletion

Last Updated: November 25, 2026 Author: Roshan Kumar (Independent Open-Source Developer)

The TL;DR

We cannot read your notes, and we never want to.
CryptoNote encrypts your notes on your phone before they ever reach our servers. All we hold in the cloud is scrambled ciphertext. We do not track you, we show zero ads, and we will never sell or monetize your data.

1. Why We Built This

Most note-taking apps store your personal thoughts, passwords, and journal entries in plain text on their company servers. Even when they claim "encryption at rest," they still hold the keys to unlock and read your files.

We built CryptoNote to work like a real safe: you hold the only key. Your master passphrase never leaves your phone. Everything is encrypted right on your device using AES-256-GCM. When you sync notes across your devices, our backend only acts as a dumb courier delivering locked boxes it cannot open.

2. What We Store

We only keep what is strictly necessary to make sync and login work:

  • StoredYour Email: Used solely to sign in, distinguish your account, and prevent unauthorized takeovers.
  • StoredAuthentication Token: A derived login hash so the server can verify your password without ever knowing what your actual password is.
  • StoredScrambled Note Envelopes: Opaque binary packages containing your encrypted note, a unique nonce, and a checksum. To our server, these look like random noise.
  • StoredSync Version Counters: Simple counter numbers (e.g. version 1, version 2) so your devices know which note edit is the newest.

3. What We Never Collect

We believe in data minimization. If we don't have it, nobody can steal it:

  • NeverYour Note Text or Titles: Plaintext never leaves your phone. We can't see your shopping lists, diary, or passwords.
  • NeverYour Master Key or Passphrase: Stored locally inside Android's hardware security chip (TEE / StrongBox Keystore). We cannot reset or recover it if you lose it.
  • NeverYour Fingerprint or Face Data: Biometric unlock is handled entirely by Android's system prompt. We never get biometric data.
  • NeverYour Location or Contacts: The app doesn't ask for location, contacts, camera, or phone storage permissions.
  • NeverAnalytics or Ad Trackers: No Google Analytics, no Facebook SDK, no advertising IDs, no trackers. Zero.

4. Infrastructure & Security

All traffic between your phone and our sync server is forced over modern HTTPS / TLS 1.3. Plaintext HTTP traffic is blocked at the operating system level inside our Android manifest.

Our backend runs on Fly.io microVMs with an isolated PostgreSQL database. That's the entire stack. We don't pipe your traffic through third-party data aggregators.

5. How to Delete Your Account & Data

You shouldn't have to jump through hoops to delete your data. You have complete control:

Option A: Instant Delete From the App

If you have your phone with you:

  1. Open CryptoNote.
  2. Tap your profile avatar in the top right to open settings.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Erase & Reset Account".
  4. Confirm the prompt.

This instantly wipes your local encryption keys and notes from your phone, and instructs our server to immediately delete your email, login records, and all encrypted backups permanently.

Option B: Delete via Email (If you uninstalled the app)

If you no longer have access to your device or already uninstalled the app:

  1. Send an email to roshankuar951@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: CryptoNote Account Deletion Request.
  3. Send it from the email address registered with your account so we can verify ownership.

Within 48 hours, we will permanently purge your account record, email, and all encrypted envelopes from our database.
