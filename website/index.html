Why CryptoNote

Privacy by design, not an afterthought

No trackers, no analytics, no ads, and no selling your data. Just a fast, reliable notes app where only you hold the keys.

Zero-Knowledge Encryption

Your note titles, text, and tags are encrypted on your phone using AES-256-GCM before anything syncs. The server only sees encrypted data and cannot read what you write.

Two Spaces: Notes & Vault

Keep everyday thoughts in Notes, and move sensitive items like passwords, backup codes, or journals into the Vault behind biometric lock.

Works 100% Offline

Write, edit, and organize notes without an internet connection. When you come back online, your changes sync in the background automatically.

Never Overwrites Your Edits

If you edit the same note on two devices while offline, CryptoNote preserves both versions side by side instead of silently overwriting one.

Screen & Memory Protection

Screenshots and task-switcher previews are blocked with Android's FLAG_SECURE. Sensitive data in the vault is cleared from memory when locked.

Clean, Familiar Design

Built with modern Material 3 styling. Choose from 7 color palettes or let it match your system wallpaper colors with true dark mode support.

Two Spaces

Everyday notes when you need speed. A secure vault when you need privacy.

Not every note needs the same level of security. CryptoNote gives you two distinct spaces so you don't have to sacrifice convenience for privacy.

For things you want extra protection for—backup codes, recovery keys, financial notes, or personal journals.

How It Works

Key Separation & Protection

Your account login password and your note encryption keys are completely separate. Changing your account password never requires re-encrypting existing notes.

Binary Envelope Format (V1)

Every encrypted note is packaged using a clean, authenticated binary format:

Field Byte Size Value Purpose
Format Version 1 Byte 0x01 Envelope binary format version
Cipher ID 1 Byte 0x01 Designates AES-256-GCM encryption
Key ID 8 Bytes HKDF-SHA256 First 8 bytes of key identifier header
Nonce (IV) 12 Bytes CSPRNG Random initialization vector generated per note
Ciphertext Variable Encrypted JSON Encrypted title, body, tags, and timestamps
GCM Auth Tag 16 Bytes 128-bit MAC Verifies note integrity and prevents tampering

Real-World Threat Protection

Server Breach / Database Leak ✓ Mitigated

The sync server only stores encrypted binary data. Without your master key (which never leaves your phone), a compromised database is completely unreadable.

Tampering in Transit ✓ Mitigated

Nobody can alter or swap your notes on the server. AES-GCM authenticated tags verify the note ID and version on every sync, rejecting any tampered data.

Lost or Stolen Device ✓ Mitigated

On-device data is encrypted with SQLCipher. Vault keys are isolated in Android's Keystore hardware and wiped from memory whenever the vault is locked.

Malicious Apps & Screen Recorders ✓ Mitigated

Android's FLAG_SECURE prevents background apps from recording your screen or capturing screenshots, and hides previews in the recent apps switcher.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about encryption, offline sync, self-hosting, and data recovery.

Yes. The sync backend is completely open source (Node.js/Fastify and PostgreSQL). You can deploy it to Fly.io, Render, or run it via Docker Compose on your own server. Just enter your custom server URL in the app's settings.

Your login password is used only to authenticate with the server. Your master encryption key is generated locally on your phone and encrypts your actual notes. Keeping them separate means you can change your server password anytime without having to re-encrypt all your notes.

When you create your account, CryptoNote provides a 256-bit recovery key. If you forget your passphrase, you can restore access with this key. If you lose both your passphrase and recovery key, no one—not even the project maintainers—can decrypt or recover your notes.
